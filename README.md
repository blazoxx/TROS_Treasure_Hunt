# Treasure Hunt – The Winter Is Coming

> A two-day, Game of Thrones–inspired campus treasure hunt for **ENYUGMA**, the annual techno-cultural fest of **IIIT Bhagalpur**.

Treasure Hunt – The Winter Is Coming was designed as a high-energy, story-driven competition where teams battled through riddles, puzzles, clues, and strategic challenges across campus. Built around the lore and rivalry of the Game of Thrones universe, the event mixed **technology, teamwork, exploration, and live gameplay** into one immersive experience.

## The Experience

The event was built to feel like a living quest.

Participants joined as houses from the Game of Thrones universe and advanced through rounds that tested more than just speed. Each stage demanded a different combination of **logic, observation, collaboration, and decision-making**. The hunt moved through multiple layers of difficulty, rewarding teams that could think quickly, communicate clearly, and stay composed under pressure.

Across both days, the competition unfolded as a progressive journey:

1. Early rounds introduced teams to the format and established momentum.
2. Mid-event stages raised the difficulty with more layered puzzles and clue chains.
3. The final stretch narrowed the field and pushed teams through the most demanding internal stages.
4. House Stark ultimately claimed victory after two intense days of gameplay.

## What Made It Special

- **Game of Thrones theme** woven into the event identity
- **Campus-wide exploration** that turned real spaces into part of the game
- **Competitive team play** that rewarded coordination and strategy
- **Progressive challenge design** that kept the event engaging from start to finish
- **Digital support through a dedicated website**, giving the hunt a modern technical layer

## Repository Structure

The codebase is organized around a modern Next.js app with reusable UI components and shared styling.

- `app/` — application routes, layout, and page entry points
- `components/` — reusable UI sections and widgets
- `public/` — static assets like fonts, sigils, music, and imagery
- `lib/` — utility helpers
- `hooks/` — shared React hooks
- `styles/` — global style definitions
